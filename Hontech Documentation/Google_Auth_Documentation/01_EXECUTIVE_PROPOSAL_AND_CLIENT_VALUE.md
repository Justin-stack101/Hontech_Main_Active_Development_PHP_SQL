## 1. Executive Summary

HonTech AutoCenter relies on fast daily operations, customer trust, and secure financial records. As the system expands from local shop computers to cloud-accessible dashboards, securing administrative accounts becomes paramount.

By integrating **Google OAuth 2.0 and Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA)**, HonTech gains enterprise-grade cybersecurity backed by Google's global infrastructure without adding any monthly software licensing costs.

## 4. Operational Peace of Mind

1. **No Stolen Password Scams**: Even if someone guesses an administrative password, they cannot access the system without the physical 2-Step Verification prompt sent to the owner's authorized phone.
2. **Instant Staff Revocation**: If an employee leaves HonTech, the Owner can deactivate their account with a single click, instantly terminating all their active sessions.
