## 1. 🎯 Executive Summary & The Core Challenge

As HonTech AutoCenter transitions from manual paper stubs into modern digital operations (Real-Time Bay Allocation, 3-Slide Lounge TV Telemetry, Multi-Branch Partitioning, and Cloud Disaster Backups), **technical systems require continuous supervision, hardware reliability, and cybersecurity governance.**

### The Problem Facing Growing Auto Centers:
- **High Cost of In-House IT**: Hiring a full-time in-house IT Department (Network Administrator, Software Engineer, Help Desk) costs **₱70,000 – ₱120,000+ PHP monthly** in salaries, benefits, and taxes.
- **Risk of Unmaintained Systems**: Without technical supervision, shop Wi-Fi networks fail, database backups get forgotten, and staff forget passwords or misuse hardware.

### The Solution: The "Fractional Dedicated IT Department"
HonTech does not need to hire full-time IT employees. Instead, **our technical team acts as your Dedicated External IT Department** for a fraction of the cost, ensuring 100% system uptime, routine backups, staff technical training, and continuous software expansion.

### Pillar 1: Software Operations & Feature Evolution
- **Continuous Maintenance**: Rapid resolution of any client browser bugs, script versioning (`v=3.x` cache-busting), and UI optimizations.
- **Feature Roadmap Delivery**: Systematic development and deployment of **Phase 2 (Spare Parts Inventory)** and **Phase 3 (POS Invoicing & Receipts)**.

### Pillar 2: Network & Hardware Infrastructure Supervision
- **On-Site Shop Server Health**: Routine health checks on the Host PC (Apache, MySQL, storage disk space).
- **Wi-Fi & LAN Inbound Routing**: Ensuring static IP bindings (`192.168.1.100`) and local domain routing (`http://hontech.local`) remain active.
- **Lounge TV Kiosk Maintenance**: Ensuring waiting room display screens auto-boot cleanly with acoustic chime notifications.

### Pillar 3: Cybersecurity, Disaster Recovery & Employee Support
- **Automated Cloud Backups**: Managing scheduled encrypted database backups to Google Drive, ensuring HonTech can recover from hardware disk crashes in <15 minutes.
- **Access Control & Staff Onboarding**: Assisting management with staff account provisioning, TOTP Google Authenticator 2FA resets, and emergency credential recovery.
- **Staff Training & Helpdesk**: Conducting hands-on workshops for new Service Advisors and Front Desk Assistants.
