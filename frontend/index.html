Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently asked questions and guides

How do I print an official Form 1/3 Job Order or Quotation?

Select the desired form tab above (Job Order, Quotation, Billing, or Checklist) and click the Print / PDF button in the top toolbar. It will open your printer dialog configured for A4 paper with zero margins distortion.

How does cross-form auto-synchronization work?

Whenever you type customer details (Name, Plate No, Contact, Model, Dates) or line items in any form, the system automatically replicates the data across all 4 forms and computes 12% BIR VAT in real time.

Can I auto-fill customer information from today's live queue?

Yes! Click the Auto-Fill from Queue dropdown in the toolbar to pick any vehicle currently in the workshop queue to instantly load their profile.
