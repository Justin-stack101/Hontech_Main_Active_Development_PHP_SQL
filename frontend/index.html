Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently asked questions and guides

How do I add a new vehicle to the Queue?

Go to the "Intake" view in the navigation, fill in the customer name, license plate, vehicle brand/model, and select the service. Once submitted, it automatically appears in the live queue dashboard.

How can I download a PDF or Word report?

In the "Dashboard" overview screen, there are dedicated export buttons at the top right of the screen. Click on "PDF" or "Word" to instantly download the generated report documents.

How do I change color themes?

Click your user profile avatar in the upper right header to expand the user dropdown menu. Under "Switch Color Theme", click on the colored circles (Teal, Blue, Purple, Orange, Red) to change colors.

User Agreement & Operational Directives

Authorized Use & Philippine Law Compliance

This digital system is deployed solely for HonTech AutoCenter workshop management and vehicle queue monitoring. By logging in, all users agree to the terms outlined below in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Credential Confidentiality

Staff accounts are strictly personal and non-transferable. Sharing credentials or using another employee's session is grounds for administrative sanction. Sessions will automatically expire after 30 minutes of inactivity.

2 Customer Data Privacy & Record Integrity

Customer contact numbers, vehicle plates, claim stubs, and PMS logs must not be disclosed or exported to unauthorized third parties. All recorded data is securely stored on local workshop infrastructure.

3 Queue Scheduling & Workshop Operations

Service Advisors (SA) and Technicians are responsible for maintaining real-time accuracy of vehicle bay locations, status changes (Waiting, Inspection, In Bay, QC, Ready), and PMS 2-Hour Goal targets.

4 Billing & Payment Boundaries

This software operates as an operational intake, queue monitoring, and analytics tracker. Official billing, payment receipts, and cash transactions must be processed via HonTech's over-the-counter accounting registry.

5 Academic & Development Disclosure

Developed in partnership with STI College Marikina BSIT Capstone Program. System logs and performance metrics may be audited for academic evaluation and maintenance purposes.
