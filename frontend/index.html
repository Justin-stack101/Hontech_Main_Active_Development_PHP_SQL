Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently asked questions and guides

How do I add a new vehicle to the Queue?

Go to the "Intake" view in the navigation, fill in the customer name, license plate, vehicle brand/model, and select the service. Once submitted, it automatically appears in the live queue dashboard.

How can I download a PDF or Word report?

In the "Dashboard" overview screen, there are dedicated export buttons at the top right of the screen. Click on "PDF" or "Word" to instantly download the generated report documents.

How do I change color themes?

Click your user profile avatar in the upper right header to expand the user dropdown menu. Under "Switch Color Theme", click on the colored circles (Teal, Blue, Purple, Orange, Red) to change colors.

User Agreement & Operational Compliance Directives

Authorized Use & Philippine Law Compliance

This digital system is deployed exclusively for HonTech AutoCenter workshop queue management and operational diagnostics. By accessing this platform, all users agree to adhere to company operating policies in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Credential Security

User accounts are assigned strictly to designated personnel (Owner, Admin, Service Advisor, Assistant) and are non-transferable. Sharing credentials or operating under another user's session is strictly prohibited. Inactive sessions automatically expire after 30 minutes.

2 Customer Privacy & Vehicle Record Confidentiality

Customer phone numbers, license plates, claim stubs, and PMS service logs are protected intellectual and private records. Unauthorized external duplication, export, or distribution of customer records is strictly forbidden under RA 10173.

3 Bay Telemetry, Queue Scheduling & 2-Hour PMS SLA

Service Advisors and Technicians must ensure real-time accuracy when updating vehicle stage transitions (Waiting, Inspection, In Bay, QC, Ready) to maintain accurate TV broadcast feeds and comply with the standard 2-hour PMS turnaround target.

4 Billing, Invoicing & Cashier Registry Boundaries

This platform serves as an operational intake, queue monitoring, and telemetry audit system. Official BIR receipts, cash collections, credit payments, and parts invoicing remain under the physical front-desk cashier registry.

5 Academic Partnership & Audit Disclosure

Developed in academic collaboration with STI College Marikina BSIT Capstone Program. Operational logs and telemetry analytics may be audited for academic evaluation and performance maintenance.
